# Title formulas (T1-T15)

Every formula below is a *shape*. Fill it with the channel's words and real receipts.
Each is tagged with the traffic source it serves best: **B** browse, **S** search, **Su** suggested.

| # | Name | Shape | Example (EN / ES) | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | Specific result + timeframe | I [did X] in [time] | "I Learned Japanese in 90 Days" / "Aprendí japonés en 90 días" | B |
| T2 | How-to, searched phrasing | How to [exact query] ([qualifier]) | "How to Edit Faster in DaVinci Resolve (2026)" | S |
| T3 | Mistake / warning | Stop [common action] (do this instead) | "Deja de ahorrar así (haz esto)" | B, Su |
| T4 | Odd-precision number | [Precise number] [thing] that [result] | "7 Settings That Doubled My Battery" | B, S |
| T5 | Curiosity gap with stakes | Why [surprising fact] | "Why Nobody Buys Electric Trucks" | B |
| T6 | Versus / comparison | [A] vs [B]: [decisive angle] | "Notion vs Obsidian After 2 Years" | S, Su |
| T7 | Challenge / constraint | I [did X] with only [constraint] | "Monté una tienda con 100 €" | B |
| T8 | The honest truth | The truth about [topic everyone sells] | "The Truth About Passive Income" | B, Su |
| T9 | Beginner path | [Topic] for beginners: [promise] | "Python para principiantes: tu primer bot" | S |
| T10 | Ranked list | Ranking every [category] | "Ranking Every Budget Mic Under $100" | B, Su |
| T11 | Tested so you don't have to | I tested [N] [things] for [time] | "I Tested 5 AI Video Editors for a Month" | B, S |
| T12 | Transformation | From [state A] to [state B] | "De 0 a 10.000 suscriptores: lo que funcionó" | B |
| T13 | Insider explanation | How [company/system] actually [does X] | "How YouTube Actually Picks Your Thumbnail" | B, Su |
| T14 | Question the viewer asks | [Question in their words]? | "¿Vale la pena un MacBook Air en 2026?" | S |
| T15 | Status / identity | What [role] [do/know] that you don't | "What Senior Devs Do in Their First Hour" | B |

## Rules

- Title and thumbnail **complement**, never repeat. If the thumbnail says "90 DAYS", the title says what happened.
- 40-60 chars is the sweet spot for browse; search titles can run longer if the query sits at the front.
- Capitalisation: follow the channel profile. English channels often use Title Case; Spanish/Portuguese use sentence case.
- One promise per title. No clickbait the video does not pay (see voice rules §10).
- Brackets/parentheses add a qualifier, not filler: "(2026)", "(sin experiencia)", "(full course)".
- Always write **at least 5 candidates across 3+ formulas**, then pick 3 for Test & Compare.
