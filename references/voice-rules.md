# Voice rules (canonical)

Every skill applies these. Skill-local "Hard rules" sections only add
skill-specific overrides and start with `Global voice rules: see root SKILL.md §Voice rules.`

## Language

1. **Reply to the user in the language they write in.** Write titles, descriptions,
   scripts and replies in the channel language from `channel-profile.md`
   (they can differ: a user may brief in English for a Spanish channel).
2. Use the viewer's register. Spain vs LatAm Spanish, tú vs usted, Brazilian vs
   European Portuguese: follow the profile, never mix inside one video.

## Spoken, not written

3. Scripts are written for the ear: short sentences, one idea each, contractions,
   concrete nouns. Read every line aloud in your head; if you would not say it
   to a friend, rewrite it.
4. No throat-clearing. Never open with "Hi guys, welcome back to the channel",
   "In today's video we are going to", or a sponsor read before the hook.

## Specific beats vague

5. Numbers, names, dates, places. "I lost 2,300 € in 11 days" beats "I lost a lot of money".
6. **Never invent receipts.** A figure, result, testimonial or credential appears only if it is
   in `channel-profile.md` §Assets & proof or the user gave it in this conversation.
   If a draft needs a number you do not have, leave `[NUMBER?]` and ask.

## AI tells to strip

7. Banned vocabulary in any language: *delve, unlock, unleash, game-changer, elevate,
   leverage, harness, seamless, robust, journey, realm, tapestry, in today's fast-paced world,
   descubre el poder de, sumérgete, en el mundo actual, potencia tu, revoluciona*.
8. No stacked triads ("fast, simple and powerful"), no "It's not X, it's Y" more than once per script,
   no rhetorical question chains, no emoji walls in descriptions.
9. Em dashes at most 1 per 100 words in written copy (descriptions, community posts).

## Honesty

10. Title + thumbnail make a promise; the first 30 seconds confirm it and the video pays it.
    No bait the video does not deliver: it wins the click and loses the session, and
    satisfaction signals punish it.
11. Disclose paid promotion, affiliate links and realistic synthetic/AI-altered content.
