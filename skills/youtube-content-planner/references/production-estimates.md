# Production estimates (solo creator, intermediate skill)

Defaults to sanity-check a calendar. Replace with the creator's real numbers when known.

| Format | Script | Film | Edit | Packaging | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Talking head 8-12 min | 2-3 h | 1-2 h | 4-8 h | 1-2 h | 8-15 h |
| Tutorial / screen recording 10-20 min | 2-4 h | 2-3 h | 3-6 h | 1-2 h | 8-15 h |
| Test / challenge / documentary 12-20 min | 3-5 h | 4-15 h | 8-20 h | 2 h | 17-42 h |
| Short from long-form clip | 0.2 h | 0 | 0.5-1 h | 0.2 h | ~1-1.5 h |
| Original Short | 0.5 h | 0.5 h | 1 h | 0.2 h | ~2 h |
| Community post (poll/teaser) | 0.2 h | | | | 0.2 h |
| Livestream 60 min | 1 h prep | 1 h | 1 h (clips) | 0.5 h | 3.5 h |

With an editor: subtract edit time, add 1 h per video for briefing and review.
