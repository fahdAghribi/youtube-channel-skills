# What makes a good Short clip

## Strong candidates
- A **counter-intuitive claim** followed by the reason
- A **specific number** with a story ("I spent 4,000 € on ads and got 3 sales")
- A **mini tutorial**: one trick, shown, done in < 40 s
- A **reaction / reveal moment** (result appears on screen)
- A **strong opinion** said plainly, with one sentence of justification
- A **mistake** the viewer recognises themselves making

## Weak candidates
- Anything that starts with "so", "and", "as I said", "like we saw before"
- Moments that depend on a visual from earlier in the video
- Long setups with the payoff after 45 s
- Sponsor segments, intros, outros

## Hook repair
Often the best first line is 2-10 seconds into the moment. Start the clip on the sentence
that carries the stakes, then add on-screen text that restates the context the viewer missed.

## Loop ending
End on a line that leads back into the first line, or cut right after the payoff so the
replay feels natural. Avoid "follow for more": it adds nothing and increases swipe-aways.
